# Design phase checklist

Tick these items before you move to development. Each group matches a chapter in the design section.

## Problem definition
- [ ] Clinical problem documented with measurable success criteria, set before seeing any data
- [ ] AI tool scoped: what it will and will not do
- [ ] Clinical need confirmed with at least one clinician from the target setting
- [ ] Reasons for choosing AI over a simpler rule or score documented

## Stakeholders
- [ ] Stakeholder map completed (clinicians, nurses, patients, managers, data protection officer, IT, legal, ethics)
- [ ] Patient engagement plan in place, with compensation and accessibility considered
- [ ] Interdisciplinary team assembled, and expertise consulted from outside the team recorded

## Intended use
- [ ] Clinical settings defined, including the resources each setting has
- [ ] User groups defined, with their role and technical literacy documented
- [ ] Explainability needs documented for each user group
- [ ] AI inputs and outputs specified
- [ ] Community-defined standards chosen for inputs and labels (for example SNOMED CT, LOINC, HL7 FHIR)
- [ ] Out-of-scope uses and settings documented

## Ethics and legal
- [ ] Type of review determined (accredited METC for WMO research, local review for non-WMO research, hospital governance for deployment) and started
- [ ] Application-specific ethical issues listed and discussed with the team
- [ ] Data governance agreements in place or in progress
- [ ] Regulatory route determined (MDR/IVDR class, whether the EU AI Act treats the tool as high-risk, in-house exemption or CE marking)
- [ ] GDPR Art. 6 lawful basis and Art. 9(2) condition for health data documented
- [ ] Data protection impact assessment (DPIA) started

## Data
- [ ] Data sources identified
- [ ] Sources of data variation documented (equipment, protocols, operators, sites, time)
- [ ] Annotation and labelling plan written
- [ ] Attributes for bias monitoring (for example age, sex, ethnicity, socioeconomic status) identified
- [ ] Legal basis and safeguards for processing these attributes documented
- [ ] Plan for later external evaluation on data from another site

## Risk management
- [ ] Risk management file opened
- [ ] Initial risk register completed with likelihood and consequence ratings
- [ ] Mitigation measures planned for high-priority risks

## Bias and social impact
- [ ] Likely sources of bias in data, labels and problem framing recorded
- [ ] Effects on staff workload and skills considered
- [ ] Effects on health equity considered
- [ ] Environmental cost of training and running the tool estimated
- [ ] Plan for telling patients that AI is used in their care
