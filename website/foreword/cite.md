# How to cite

To cite The ELSA Way as a whole, use:

> The ELSA Way Community. *The ELSA Way: A Community Resource for Responsible AI in Healthcare*. ELSA AI Lab Northern Netherlands, 2026. Available at: [https://the-elsa-way.github.io/the-elsa-way/](https://the-elsa-way.github.io/the-elsa-way/)

The book changes over time, so when you cite a single page, add its title and the date you accessed it:

> The ELSA Way Community. "Fairness and bias assessment." In *The ELSA Way: A Community Resource for Responsible AI in Healthcare*. ELSA AI Lab Northern Netherlands, accessed [date]. Available at: [https://the-elsa-way.github.io/the-elsa-way/fairness-bias](https://the-elsa-way.github.io/the-elsa-way/fairness-bias)

## The FUTURE-AI framework

If you refer to the FUTURE-AI framework, please also cite the original paper:

> Lekadir K, Frangi AF, Porras AR, et al. FUTURE-AI: international consensus guideline for trustworthy and deployable artificial intelligence in healthcare. *BMJ* 2025;388:e081554. doi:10.1136/bmj-2024-081554
