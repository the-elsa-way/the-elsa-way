# The FUTURE-AI framework

The ELSA Way is built on FUTURE-AI, an international consensus guideline for trustworthy AI in healthcare published in the BMJ in 2025 {cite}`lekadir2025futureai`. A consortium of 117 experts from 50 countries agreed on 30 recommendations, grouped under six principles whose initials spell the name: fairness, universality, traceability, usability, robustness and explainability, plus seven general recommendations.

We chose FUTURE-AI for four reasons. It reflects broad international agreement rather than one organisation or country. It covers the whole lifecycle, including the deployment work that technical guidelines often skip. It rates each recommendation separately for research prototypes and for tools meant for routine care, so you can see what applies to your stage. And it works alongside reporting guidelines such as TRIPOD+AI {cite}`tripodai2024` and CLAIM {cite}`claim2020`: those tell you what to report about a study, FUTURE-AI tells you what to do while you build the tool, and its documentation recommendation (T2) points you to the reporting guidelines.

The [FUTURE-AI principles](../future-ai/future-ai.md) section has a page for each principle. The [FUTURE-AI overview](../future-ai/overview.md) lists all 30 recommendations with their compliance levels and the chapter that covers each one.
