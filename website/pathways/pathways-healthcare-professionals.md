# Pathway: Healthcare professionals

This pathway is for doctors, nurses, allied health professionals and clinical leads who use AI tools or help choose them. You know the ward, the clinic and the patients. The pages below show where that knowledge is needed: in deciding what a tool is for, in testing whether it fits your work, and in checking that it keeps working once it is in use.
