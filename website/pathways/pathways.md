# Pathways

A ward nurse, a data scientist and a hospital board member need different parts of this book. Each pathway picks the pages that matter most for one role and puts them in a reading order:

- [AI developers](pathways-ai-developers.md)
- [Healthcare professionals](pathways-healthcare-professionals.md)
- [Patients and communities](pathways-patients-communities.md)
- [Researchers](pathways-researchers.md)
- [Policy makers and managers](pathways-policy-managers.md)

:::{admonition} Running case: sepsis early warning
:class: note
This book follows a fictional example. A regional hospital in the Northern Netherlands builds a model that estimates sepsis risk every hour from data in its electronic health record and alerts the ward nurse and the physician on call. Its data scientists would follow the AI developers pathway, its nurses and internists the healthcare professionals pathway, the members of its patient advisory panel the patients and communities pathway, the university partner the researchers pathway, and the board and the medical technology department the policy makers and managers pathway.
:::

You do not have to read a pathway from start to finish. Skip the pages you already know and follow the cross-references when you want more depth. Each pathway ends with tools from the Toolbox that suit the role.
