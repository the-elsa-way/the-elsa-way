# Toolbox

The chapters of The ELSA Way explain _what_ responsible AI in healthcare asks of you. The Toolbox collects the practical tools that help you _do_ it: workshop methods, serious games, impact assessments, reporting guidelines and software libraries.

You will also meet these tools in the chapters themselves. Wherever a tool helps with a specific step, a **Tools for this step** box appears in that section. Each tool has its own page with practical details: how long it takes, whether you need a facilitator, what it costs, its licence, and where else in the book it is useful.

:::{note} How tools are selected
A tool is included when it is relevant to healthcare AI, supports at least one FUTURE-AI principle, and is publicly available from its developers. Descriptions are written by The ELSA Way community. We link to each tool rather than copy it, unless its licence allows reuse. Read [Adding a Tool](#toolbox-contributing) to suggest one.
:::
